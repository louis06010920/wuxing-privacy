遊戲資料與裝置權限

本遊戲不要求建立遊戲帳戶。遊戲進度、設定、語言、隨機產生的本機安裝識別碼及購買授權憑證儲存在您的裝置，用於保存進度與確認已解鎖內容，不上傳至開發者伺服器。本機安裝識別碼並非手機硬體序號。

本遊戲沒有廣告、廣告追蹤或第三方使用行為分析 SDK，不讀取您的聯絡人、位置、相機或麥克風，也不出售個人資料。

平台購買服務

完整版本的一次性購買及恢復購買由 Google Play Billing 處理。Google 依其服務條款及隱私權政策處理付款與交易資料。本遊戲取得商品與購買狀態、購買憑證及簽章，以驗證及保存解鎖資格；不取得您的完整信用卡號或付款密碼。開發者可透過 Google Play Console 處理訂單與退款。

iOS 版本提供 App 內購買時，由 Apple App Store 處理付款與恢復購買。遊戲僅使用商品、交易與購買狀態確認解鎖資格，不取得完整信用卡號或付款密碼。退款依 Apple 的申請流程處理。參閱 Google 隱私權政策與 Apple 隱私權政策。

遊戲支援與聯絡客服 / Game Support

遊戲操作、畫面或購買問題，請來信並提供裝置型號、系統版本、遊戲版本、問題重現步驟；可附上不含個人敏感資料的截圖。本遊戲不需註冊帳號。Android 與 iOS 的購買資格由各自商店管理，不支援跨平台移轉。

For gameplay, display or purchase issues, email us with your device model, OS and game version, and steps to reproduce the problem. Screenshots are welcome; please omit sensitive information. No game account is required. Purchases are managed separately by each platform and do not transfer between Android and iOS.

您若主動寄信至 wuxinginkdefense@gmail.com，我們會使用您的寄件地址、信件內容及自願提供的附件來回覆問題、處理支援或退款相關請求。請勿傳送密碼或完整付款資訊。客服資料僅在處理請求及必要的後續紀錄期間保留；您可來信要求刪除，依法必須保留的紀錄除外。

保留、刪除與安全

本機資料會保留至您清除應用程式資料或解除安裝；這也可能刪除遊戲進度。裝置系統備份若有啟用，依系統供應商的設定處理。Google Play 與 App Store 購買紀錄分別由 Google 與 Apple 管理，解除安裝不會刪除購買紀錄，也不代表退款。重新安裝後可使用原購買帳戶恢復符合資格的購買。

我們採取合理措施保護客服及授權資料，但無法保證任何裝置、網路或儲存方式絕對安全。您可透過下列信箱提出資料存取、更正或刪除請求。

Wuxing Ink Defense — Privacy Policy

Developer: GreatOfGood
Android / iOS identifier: com.wuxing.inkdefense
Effective: October 14, 2026

Local game data

No game account is required. Progress, preferences, language, a randomly generated installation identifier and purchase credentials are stored on your device to preserve progress and verify access. They are not uploaded to a developer-operated server. The identifier is not a hardware serial number. The game contains no advertising or third-party behavioral analytics SDK, does not access contacts, location, camera or microphone, and does not sell personal data.

Purchases

Google Play Billing processes the one-time full-game purchase and restoration under Google's own terms and privacy policy. The game receives product and purchase status, purchase credentials and signatures to verify and retain entitlement. It does not receive full card numbers or payment passwords. The developer may manage orders and refunds through Play Console. When in-app purchases are offered on iOS, Apple App Store processes payments and restoration. The game uses product, transaction and purchase status to verify entitlement and does not receive full card numbers or payment passwords. Refund requests follow Apple's process. See Google's Privacy Policy and Apple's Privacy Policy.

Support and retention

If you email wuxinginkdefense@gmail.com, we use your email address, message and voluntary attachments to respond and resolve support or purchase issues. Do not send passwords or full payment information. Support records are retained for resolving requests and necessary follow-up records. You may request access, correction or deletion by email, subject to legally required retention.

Local data remains until you clear app data or uninstall, which may remove progress. Any OS backup is governed by your device provider's settings. Google and Apple manage their respective purchase records; uninstalling is not a refund and does not erase those records. Eligible purchases can be restored with the original purchasing account. We use reasonable safeguards but cannot guarantee absolute security.

Age, hosting and changes

The game is intended for ages 13 and above, is not directed to children under 13, and does not knowingly collect their personal information. Parents may contact us about support information submitted by a child.
