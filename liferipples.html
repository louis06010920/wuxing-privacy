生命波紋 — 隱私政策與支援

開發者：GreatOfGood。生效日期：2026-10-16。本政策適用於 Android 與 iOS 版 org.liferipples.game。

本機遊戲資料

不需要註冊遊戲帳號。關卡進度、星星、提示次數、語言與聲音設定儲存在裝置本機，不傳送至開發者伺服器，沒有跨裝置進度同步。清除應用程式資料或解除安裝可能移除本機進度。

購買與恢復

Google Play 處理付款。遊戲使用商品識別碼、購買狀態、購買憑證及其簽章驗證與恢復完整版權限，必要憑證保存在裝置本機。遊戲不取得信用卡號碼。相同 Google Play 帳號可恢復已購商品。Google 依其政策處理交易紀錄；清除本機遊戲資料不會刪除 Google 的交易紀錄。

iOS 購買與恢復

iOS 版由 Apple App Store 處理付款，透過 StoreKit 驗證購買狀態與恢復完整版權限。遊戲不取得信用卡號碼，不將交易資料傳送至開發者伺服器。可使用購買時相同的 Apple 帳號恢復購買。Apple 依其政策處理交易及診斷資料，刪除遊戲不會刪除 Apple 交易紀錄。iOS 本機進度可透過刪除 App 清除（卸載 App 可能保留資料）。詳見 Apple 隱私權政策。

廣告、分析及權限

本版沒有廣告 SDK、行為分析 SDK 或開發者資料收集伺服器，不要求定位、聯絡人、相機或麥克風權限。Google Play 與作業系統可能依自身政策及設定處理交易與診斷資料。

資料刪除與客服

本機資料可在 Android 應用程式設定中清除。以電子郵件聯絡客服時，我們會使用來信內容及信箱回覆問題，不用於廣告行銷。你可來信要求刪除客服往來資料，但依法或為處理爭議必須保留的資料除外。

Google 隱私政策

Life Ripples — Privacy Policy and Support

Developer: GreatOfGood. Effective October 16, 2026. Applies to Android and iOS app org.liferipples.game.

Local game data

No game account is required. Progress, stars, hint stock and preferences stay on your device and are not sent to a developer server. There is no cross-device progress synchronization. Clearing app data or uninstalling may remove local progress.

Purchases

Google Play handles payments. Product identifiers, purchase status and signed receipts are used to verify and restore full-game access, with required receipts stored locally. The game does not obtain card numbers. Purchases may be restored using the same Google Play account. Deleting local data does not delete Google transaction records.

iOS purchases and restoration

Apple App Store processes iOS payments. StoreKit verifies purchase status and restores full-game access. The game does not receive card numbers or send transaction data to a developer server. Restore using the same Apple Account used for purchase. Apple processes transactions and diagnostics under its own policies; deleting the game does not delete Apple transaction records. Delete the app to remove local iOS progress; offloading may retain data. See Apple Privacy Policy.

Advertising and permissions

No advertising or behavioral analytics SDK or developer data collection server is included. No location, contacts, camera or microphone access is requested. Google Play and the operating system may process transaction and diagnostic data under their own policies and settings.

Deletion and support

Delete local data in Android app settings. If you email support, your address and message are used to respond, not for advertising. You may request deletion of support correspondence except where retention is required by law or to resolve a dispute.
